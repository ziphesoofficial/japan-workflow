EDITOR WORKFLOW

Your Daily Content Workflow

A visual walkthrough of how every video goes from a raw idea to a finished, uploaded short. Follow this order every day.

1

🔍 Research & Idea

Find a topic, clip, or story with real viral potential — not just "interesting," but something people will stop scrolling for.

  • Pull from TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook
  • Study our page's top-performing videos first
  • Stay inside our niche — don't drift off-topic
  • Daily pair: 1 food video + 1 general Japan video (see Daily Content Mix)
💡 Ask: would I stop scrolling for this?
2

✍️ Script

Write the story from scratch with a strong hook and clear narrative arc.

  • Hook must land in the first 1–3 seconds
  • Keep it tight — 15–40 seconds total runtime
  • Build curiosity, don't front-load the payoff
3

🎙️ Voiceover

Create or arrange a voiceover that matches the story's pacing and keeps viewers locked in.

  • Clear, energetic delivery — no monotone
  • Sync pacing with visual cuts
4

🎞️ Visuals

Find relevant clips and supporting footage that fit the story you've built.

  • Source from across platforms
  • Prioritize footage that supports the hook's promise
5

✂️ Edit

Transform raw material into a polished, fast-paced final video.

  • Captions, sound effects, music, transitions
  • No dead air — constant movement and pacing
  • Vertical 9:16 format only
💡 Should feel new, not "lightly edited clips"
6

📤 Upload & Submit

Deliver the finished video to the shared Google Drive.

  • Minimum 2 videos/day
  • Deadline: 8:00 PM EST
  • Label files clearly, set sharing to "Anyone with the link"
  • Send the Drive link once uploaded
  • Weekly: 1 long-form video (7–30 min) — mandatory
⏰ Daily deadline: 8:00 PM EST

⏱️ Anatomy of a Short

Every video follows the same four beats. Timings scale with your runtime (15–40 seconds).

🪝

0–3s · Hook

Strongest visual plus short on-screen text. Promise something surprising and point at the detail.

🧩

3–10s · Build

Give just enough context to keep curiosity alive. Short captions, no dead air, tease the payoff.

💥

10–30s · Payoff

Deliver the surprising part you promised. Change the visual every 1–3 seconds.

🔁

Last 2–3s · Close

A short call-to-action or a loop line that sends viewers back to the start.

🚫 Common Mistakes to Avoid

These are real mistakes editors have made before — study them so you don't repeat them.

1. Caption / Font Placement

Rule: Text overlays must be centered and use font size 12. Off-center or inconsistent sizing looks unpolished and hurts retention.

✅ Correct: centered, clean, easy to read at a glance

2. Dead Air in the Voiceover

Rule: No dead air — the voiceover should talk at a fast, upbeat pace that keeps energy high throughout. If you're using ElevenLabs, use a voice similar to "Alex - Upbeat, Energetic and Clear" as your pacing reference.

3. Weak Visual Hooks

Rule: The visual hook (what's on screen in the first 1–3 seconds) should immediately make viewers curious — not just show a title card. It should tease something unexpected before the voiceover even explains it.

📝 Scripting Inspiration (Not Copying)

Use other creators' videos as a reference for pacing and structure only. The goal is to learn how other creators build a script — the rhythm, the reveal, the phrasing style — then write your own original version in your own words. Never copy a script line-for-line.

✅ Do: Borrow the structure (hook → build-up → payoff) and pacing rhythm.
🚫 Don't: Reuse the same sentences, phrasing, or exact wording.
